# L5 — Decision layer and refusal logic

Reviewer L5, 2026-10-09. Repo HEAD b313006 + uncommitted doc edits. Read-only; nothing was executed (all claims below are from reading code and tests, not from runs).

## Verdict

1. The decision layer is honest about being unfinished: with the built-in policies `recommended_under_policy` is unreachable by construction (policy.py:54-64, :312), so the package never recommends; it abstains, which matches RELEASE.md:11 and docs/validation_results.md:95.
2. The refusal vocabulary (below_design_floor, non_identifiable, insufficient_evidence, not_needed, rejected_distortion) is clear and deterministic; the weighted median, sorted iteration and seeded permutation make decisions reproducible.
3. The headline "held-out" guarantee is weaker than its text: the policy checks that the validate split could *contain* both batches, but every metric is computed on all frozen cells from all splits, including the training samples and the CytoNorm control aliquots (L5-01).
4. Guardrails fail open when a guardrail metric is missing, and the test that is meant to prove otherwise cannot fail (L5-03).
5. The "no unacceptable preservation failure" gate is implemented only as per-batch MAD compression and correlation drift; the Stage 8 biological-preservation metrics are not wired into the pipeline or policy (L5-04).
6. Refusal is design-wide only: a contrast that is non-identifiable is silently dropped from evidence and from the coverage denominator unless *all* contrasts are non-identifiable (L5-05).
7. Handoff B3 is confirmed open in code: in-split CytoNorm control insufficiency is `not_available` (stage status "blocked"), the design-audit eligibility manifest calls CytoNorm "eligible" on a layout the adapter then refuses, and split proportions are not configurable (L5-02, L5-06).
8. Thresholds are all "uncalibrated engineering default"; one (MAD compression fraction) is magnitude-blind and may over-reject real correctors (L5-07). No real-data calibration exists.
9. Counts: 0 blocker, 6 major (L5-01..05, 07), 7 minor, 1 nit.

## What is solid

- Split design is leakage-conscious: partition unit is declared and recorded, falls back donor -> biospecimen -> sample with an explicit weak-independence warning; whole biospecimens move together; assignments independent of arrival order; invariant check against sample overlap; empty split and missing-arm cases refuse or warn visibly (splits.py:250-291, 528-545, 571-606; `split_members` re-validates the frozen artefact and rejects overlap, :660-689).
- CytoNorm adapter refuses to fit on an unbalanced partition, on training controls in <2 batches, or when a training reference's donor appears in validation (cytonorm.py:219-288); `fit_apply_verified` is gated by an executed test.
- Policy has no weighted score; abstention and "uncorrected is valid" are first-class; identity candidate always retained; clustering-derived labels cannot support a recommendation under the conservative policy (policy.py:288-300).
- Reductions use the identical comparison set in numerator and denominator, so dropping a failed comparison cannot raise the score, and an undefined denominator is not epsilon-stabilised (deltas.py:1-7, 70-84).
- Design audit works at sample level, treats biological pseudo-controls as non-references (audit.py:24-26), separates reference-supported from conditional, and gives method eligibility rules with cited upstream origin; perfect confounding is caught and blocks cyCombine with `override_allowed=False` (audit.py:42-115, 342).
- Override is recorded before the backend runs so a crash cannot erase it (pipeline.py:285-296).
- Reproducibility of decisions: weighted-median with fixed sort, `sorted(set(...))` iteration, seeded numpy only, no timestamps in policy; same inputs give the same states.
- Status documents (blocked.md:180-187, implementation_status.md:235, validation_results.md:95) disclose the B3 gap and the uncalibrated-policy limit accurately.
